Club Distance

Stop guessing between clubs

Every shot you log builds a real average — not the number printed on the club. See all three swing lengths side by side and spot overlaps at a glance.

  • Full, ½ and ¼ swing — wedge yardages for every scoring shot.
  • Gapping chart — every club on one distance scale.
  • Auto-logged on course — shots measured during a round feed straight into your averages.
  • Yards or meters, and your own bag — add, rename or reorder clubs.

Scoreboard

Your caddie on every hole

Start a round, set the pin once, and Golf Caddie does the math — distance to the flag from where you stand, how far each shot went, and which club fits.

Satellite course map
Tee, pin, shot path and 50–200 yd rings on real imagery.
GPS shot marking
One tap marks the ball; or tap the map to measure any point.
Club suggestion
Matches the remaining distance to your own averages.
Full scorecard
Score, putts, penalties, out/in totals and to-par.
